Questions we get

Is this affiliated with that other site?

No. This is a joke about flies, made by someone who thought of the name first and the website second. No affiliation with, endorsement by, or resemblance to any real subscription platform is intended.

Is there any adult content?

There is a fly eating a peach it found behind a radiator. That is as far as it goes. Everything here is safe for work, assuming your work is fine with flies.

How do creators get paid?

Crumbs. Deposited on the sill. We have been told this is not a payment processor and we are appealing.

Can I become a creator?

Applications are open to anyone with compound eyes, four thousand of them, and a willingness to walk on things before you eat them. Human applications are automatically forwarded to the bin, where a creator will review them.

What's the average lifespan of a creator?

28 days. Please be kind in the comments, they are working on a deadline.
